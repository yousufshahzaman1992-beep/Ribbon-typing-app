Who this practice is for

This guide is aimed at candidates preparing for clerical and civil service roles that include a typing or data-entry component. The exact format varies by exam and authority, so always confirm the current rules for your specific notification.

  • Candidates building speed before a Hindi or English typing test.
  • Anyone who needs to practise without backspace in strict mode.
  • Typists returning to practice after a break and rebuilding rhythm.

A simple weekly routine

Day 1-2: accuracy

Slow timed passages with exam mode on. Do not chase WPM yet.

Day 3-4: speed

Same passages, slightly faster, while keeping errors low.

Day 5: mixed script

Alternate Hindi and English so neither layout feels unfamiliar.

Day 6: full test

One uninterrupted run, then review every missed character.

What to track

Track two numbers only: words per minute and accuracy. Progress in one without the other is not progress. If accuracy drops, slow down for a session. If accuracy is high but speed is flat, add short bursts at a slightly faster pace.

Consistency beats intensity. Four short sessions a week will move your score further than one long session.

Frequently asked questions

Can I practise both Hindi and English here?

Yes. Ribbon includes English and Devanagari Hindi passages, so you can alternate scripts in the same session.

Is strict exam mode available?

Yes. Strict mode disables backspace so you practise forward-only typing, similar to many government tests.

Does this guarantee a pass?

No. It is practice software, not an official exam. Always check your exam authority for the current pattern and scoring rules.

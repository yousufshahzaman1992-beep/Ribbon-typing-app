Why games help (and where they don't)

The skill gain always comes from correct, repeated finger movement. Games do not change that — they give you two things a plain drill lacks: a reason to keep practising, and a pace that pushes you slightly past comfortable. That is genuinely useful, but it is not a substitute for learning the layout.

  • Games help with: consistency, reaction speed, confidence under a timer, and motivation on days you do not feel like drilling.
  • Games do not fix: unknown finger assignments, a keyboard you have not learned, or accuracy you have not built. For those, use the structured lessons first.
  • Best combination: a short lesson or drill to learn the movement, then a game to practise it at speed.

How to use them without building bad habits

  1. Warm up first. Spend two minutes on a lesson or drill before you play, so the game practises correct movements rather than guesses.
  2. Watch accuracy, not the score. If accuracy drops below roughly 95%, slow down or move to an easier mode.
  3. Raise difficulty gradually. Increase bot speed or arcade pace only after you clear the current setting comfortably.
  4. Play in short bursts. Ten to fifteen minutes is plenty. Fatigue produces mistakes, and mistakes get practised too.
  5. Finish with a timed test. Use the 60-second test to confirm the game time is showing up in your real WPM.

Frequently asked questions

Do typing games actually improve your typing speed?

Yes, if they make you repeat accurate keystrokes. Games add pace and motivation, which helps consistency, but the underlying gain comes from correct finger movement. Ribbon mixes games with structured lessons so play translates into measurable WPM.

Are the typing games free?

All of them are free and ad-free. There is no paywall, no sign-up and no in-game purchase. You can play the race, arcade and weekly challenge modes as often as you like.

Can I play the typing games offline?

Yes. Install Ribbon as a progressive web app and the built-in games, races and challenges keep working without an internet connection. Only the shared leaderboard needs a connection.

What is the best typing game for beginners?

Start with slower bot races and the beginner lessons rather than arcade mode. Races give a clear pace target without overwhelming you, and you can raise the bot speed as your accuracy improves.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Ribbon Typing Coach ("we", "the app") is a free typing speed test and practice tool. This page explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Data stored on your device

The app is designed to work primarily in your browser. The following information is stored locally on your device (in your browser's local storage) and is never sent to our servers:

  • Typing statistics, such as words-per-minute (WPM), accuracy, and session history
  • Progress, streaks, achievements, experience points, and weekly challenge results
  • Your chosen display name (for leaderboards and challenge links)
  • Preferences such as keyboard layout, language, and theme

2. Optional AI-generated content

When you request an AI-generated story or drill, a text prompt describing your request is sent to a third-party AI provider to generate the content. The prompt may include the topic you chose and the selected language. It does not include your name or other personal identifiers, and generated content is not associated with your account.

3. Sharing and challenges

If you share a "challenge" link, the link contains only a display name, your WPM score, accuracy, and duration. Anyone with the link can see that information.

4. Analytics and cookies

The app does not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, and pages requested) may be collected for security and operational purposes and are not sold or shared with third parties.

5. Children's privacy

The app may be used by students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not create user accounts or collect contact information. All personalization is stored on the user's own device.

6. Your choices

You can clear all locally stored data at any time using the "Clear Telemetry Profiles" option in the app's settings, or by clearing your browser's site data. This will remove your history, progress, and preferences from that device.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.

Why an ad-free typing test gives you better scores

Typing speed is a measurement of rhythm. Anything that interrupts your rhythm — a banner that pops in, a layout that shifts, a cookie consent wall — costs you words per minute and, worse, breaks your flow. A distraction-free typing test produces a more honest number and a more repeatable one, so you can actually tell whether your practice is working.

No layout shift while you type

The text block stays put. The timer, the WPM counter and the character you are on never jump because an ad loaded underneath them.

No consent wall or pop-ups

There is nothing to dismiss. Open the page, start typing, and see your result.

No autoplay audio

Sound effects are opt-in only. Nothing plays until you choose to enable it.

No newsletter nags

Your result is never held hostage in exchange for an email address.

What you get with Ribbon

"Ad-free" does not mean stripped down. The coach is genuinely complete, and everything below is free with no account:

  • A 60-second timed WPM test with live words-per-minute, accuracy and error counts.
  • 113 guided lessons, from pure home-row drills for absolute beginners to full paragraphs and classic literature.
  • Hindi (Devanagari) practice, including SSC CHSL and CGL exam-style passages with a strict exam mode that disables backspace.
  • JavaScript and code practice with auto-brackets, indentation and symbol tracking.
  • Dvorak and Colemak layouts for anyone switching away from QWERTY.
  • Typing games — arcade challenges, bot races and a weekly challenge with a global leaderboard.
  • Local progress analytics, so you can watch your WPM trend improve over time.

Your data stays on your device

Many "free" tools monetise the thing you came to measure. Ribbon does the opposite. Your typing statistics, streaks, achievements and preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. There is no account, so there is nothing to breach and nothing to sell. The one optional exception is an AI story request, where the topic you type is sent to a language model to generate practice text — never your results or your identity.

Clearing your browser's site data (or using "Clear Telemetry Profiles" in Settings) removes everything Ribbon has stored about you. That is the whole footprint.

How to take the test

  1. Open the coach. No download, no login — it loads straight into the typing area.
  2. Type the passage. Your live WPM, accuracy and error count update as you go.
  3. Read your result. You get speed and accuracy together, because a fast but sloppy run is not a good run.
  4. Practice deliberately. Jump into a lesson that targets the keys you missed, then test again.

Frequently asked questions

Is this typing test really ad-free?

Yes. Ribbon shows no banner ads, video ads, pop-ups or sponsored placements. The typing area is a single clean column so nothing moves or flashes while you are being measured.

Do I need an account or an email address?

No. There is no sign-up, no login and no email required. Your results, streaks and lesson progress are saved in your own browser, not on a server.

Does the app track me across the web?

No. Ribbon does not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. Your typing statistics stay on your device, and the app does not build a profile about you.

Can I use the typing test without internet?

Yes. Ribbon is an installable progressive web app. Once you have opened it, it can be installed and used offline for timed tests, lessons and games.

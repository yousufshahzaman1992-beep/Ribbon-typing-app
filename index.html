Frequently asked questions

How is typing speed (WPM) measured?
Your WPM is calculated from the characters you type correctly each minute. A standard word is five characters, so a 60-second test with 300 correct characters equals 60 WPM. Accuracy is the share of keystrokes you get right and counts toward your score alongside speed.

Is the typing speed test free?
Yes. The 60-second WPM test, every practice lesson, the weekly challenge, and all arcade mini-games are completely free with no account required. Progress is saved locally and the app works offline as an installable PWA.

What typing content can I practice?
English and Hindi (Devanagari) practice, JavaScript code typing with automatic bracket handling, medical and complex terminology drills, custom stories and pasted text, plus timed story races, zen freestyle, and strict exam mode with backspace disabled.

How do I improve my words per minute?
Practice daily, focus on accuracy before speed, use the on-screen hand guide to build correct finger placement, and retake short timed tests. The app tracks streaks, XP, and level so you can see steady improvement, and the weekly challenge lets you compare your WPM against other typists on the global leaderboard.

Can I compare my typing speed with others?
Yes. Finish a session to open the shared weekly leaderboard, where your best score for the current week is stored under your chosen name. You can also generate a challenge link and share it so friends can race the same passage and try to beat your WPM.

Does Ribbon Typing Coach work on my phone?
Yes. Ribbon is fully responsive with an on-screen keyboard for tablets and touch devices, and it can be installed as a progressive web app so it runs offline in fullscreen like a native app.
